Long ago, in a town in Hausaland, people lived their lives as they always had. During the day, the streets were busy, people went about their work, and the town was full of life.

But when night came, something changed.

There was a creature known as Dodo.

Dodo was feared, and the people knew better than to challenge it.

One night, Dodo came into the town.

As it moved through the darkness, it called out loudly:

“In this town, who is able to fight with me?”

Its voice carried through the streets.

The people heard it, but nobody dared to answer.

Doors were shut. People hid wherever they could. Some were so frightened that they climbed into their grain bins and stayed there, hoping the creature would not find them.

Dodo continued its challenge.

“Who is able to fight with me?”

But the town remained silent.

Except for one person.

His name was Auta.

Auta was a young boy who was staying with an old woman. He was a stranger in the town, lodging in her home.

When he heard Dodo's challenge, he answered:

“I am.”

The old woman could hardly believe what she had heard.

She asked him if he had lost his senses and begged him to come inside. She knew how dangerous Dodo was and could not understand why a young boy would willingly challenge such a creature.

But Auta did not retreat.

Instead, he went to the fire.

He took seven stones and placed them in the fire, allowing them to become extremely hot.

He waited.

Before long, Dodo approached the house.

The creature came right up to the entrance. It was so large that it had to bend down to get through the low doorway.

That was the moment Auta had been waiting for.

As Dodo stooped to enter, Auta took one of the hot stones and threw it straight into the creature's mouth.

Dodo swallowed it.

The creature backed out of the doorway and stood upright again.

But it was not finished.

Once more, Dodo called out:

“Who in this town can fight with me?”

And once more, Auta answered.

The creature returned to the doorway.

Again, it bent down to enter.

Again, Auta threw a hot stone into its mouth.

Dodo swallowed another.

This happened repeatedly.

Each time Dodo challenged the town, Auta answered. Each time the creature approached the doorway, Auta threw another burning stone into its mouth.

One stone.

Then another.

Until all seven stones were gone.

Eventually, Dodo could no longer continue.

By the time morning came, the creature was dead.

THE FOLLOWING MORNING

At dawn, the women of the town came out to fetch water.

They discovered Dodo's body lying in the street.

The sight frightened them, and they raised the alarm.

Word quickly spread through the town.

The king sent horsemen to investigate the creature, but something remarkable happened.

Their horses refused to approach Dodo's body.

No matter what the horsemen did, the animals would not go near it.

Nobody could understand how the creature had been defeated.

Then Auta came forward.

He had taken Dodo's tail during the night, and he presented it as proof that he was the one who had killed the creature.

The people now knew who had answered Dodo's challenge.

It was Auta, the young stranger who had been staying with the old woman.

The king rewarded him for his bravery.

And that was how Auta defeated the terrifying Dodo using seven hot stones.
